Before You Use This

This is a decision-support prototype, not a certified stowage authority. Every plan it produces satisfies the physical rules listed below, but a plan that passes every check can still be unshippable in practice. Verify against your carrier's requirements before loading anything.

  • Containers are modelled as empty cuboids — no corrugation, wheel wells, dents, lashing rings or reefer intrusions. Air ULDs are the exception: their angled side is modelled, but as a stepped approximation of the real curve.
  • All cargo is treated as a rectangular box. Drums, pipes and sacks are packed as their bounding box, so round items are never nested.
  • Support is measured by contact area (≥ 75%), not centre of mass. A box supported at one end can pass this test and still tip in reality.
  • No load securing. Dunnage, bracing, lashing and airbag space are not reserved, and there is no axle-load, hazmat-segregation or temperature model.
  • Freight costs are illustrative market figures, not quotes. Replace them with your own before drawing any commercial conclusion.
  • The 75% support threshold and the 0.5 cm tolerance are chosen engineering values, not values derived from a standard.

Full assumptions, scoring terms and rejection reasons are in the ⓘ help inside the planner.
